# Home Story

A web app for real estate agents to create beautiful, book-like digital 
experiences for homes they are selling. The agent builds the story, 
the buyer reads it and feels connected to the home.

## Two screens
- /builder — agent inputs photos, address, and custom chapters
- /story — the rendered storybook output, cinematic and beautiful

## Aesthetic
Hardcover book feel. Warm, emotional, luxury. Not SaaS.
Cream paper tones, gold accents, serif typography.

## Key rule
No demographic language anywhere (Fair Housing compliance).
